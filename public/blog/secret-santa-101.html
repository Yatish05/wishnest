Secret Santa 101: How to Organize One Your Office Will Actually Enjoy

By WishNest • 4 min read

We all know how an office Secret Santa usually goes. You draw the name of someone from accounting you've spoken to exactly twice. You panic, buy them a generic mug with a mildly funny pun on it, and watch them fake a smile at the holiday party.

It doesn't have to be this way! With a few modern tweaks and the right Secret Santa wishlist app, you can turn a dreaded corporate obligation into a genuinely fun event.

1. Make Participation Strictly Optional

The fastest way to ruin a Secret Santa is to force everyone to participate. Some people are on tight budgets, some don't celebrate the holidays, and some just don't like gift exchanges. Send out an opt-in form, and only draw names among the people who excitedly check "Yes."

2. Set a Firm, Realistic Budget

A budget of ₹500 is common, but often results in cheap gag gifts that end up in the trash. Consider raising the limit slightly to ₹1000, allowing people to buy something of actual quality—like a good book or nice coffee beans. Whatever the limit is, make sure it is strictly enforced so no one feels awkward for over- or under-spending.

3. Mandate the Use of a Wishlist

The biggest stress of Secret Santa is guessing what your coworker likes. To eliminate this, have everyone create a digital list using a free tool like WishNest. They can link exactly what they want within the budget. It guarantees everyone gets something they will use and love.

If you don't want to mandate links, at least have everyone fill out a "Likes and Dislikes" sheet (e.g., Favorite snack, Coffee or Tea order, Hobbies).

4. Use an Online Name Generator

Drawing names out of a hat in the breakroom is outdated, especially with hybrid and remote teams. Use an online generator that automatically emails each person their assigned recipient. This keeps everything anonymous and prevents people from drawing their own names!
