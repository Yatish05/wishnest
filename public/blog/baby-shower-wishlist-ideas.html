Baby Shower Wishlist Ideas: What to Actually Register For

By WishNest • 5 min read

Walking into a baby store (or browsing one online) as an expectant parent is terrifying. Do you really need a wipe warmer? What is a Diaper Genie? Why are there six different types of swaddles?

If you are building your registry with a baby shower wishlist app, it’s easy to go overboard and add 200 items. But the secret to a great registry is keeping it focused on what you will actually use in the first year. Here are the core things to include.

1. The Big Ticket Essentials

These are the expensive items that friends or family might want to purchase together as a group gift (see our guide on how to organize a group gift). Make sure these are well-researched:

  • A convertible car seat
  • A lightweight, easily foldable stroller
  • A safe crib and a high-quality mattress
  • A comfortable nursing chair or glider for the nursery

2. The Practical Daily Items

People love buying cute baby outfits, but babies outgrow newborn sizes in weeks. Instead, fill your list with practical daily drivers:

  • Muslin swaddle blankets (they double as burp cloths, nursing covers, and stroller shades)
  • A highly rated digital thermometer
  • Fingernail clippers or an electric nail file designed for babies
  • A portable white noise machine

3. Gifts for the Parents!

People often forget that the parents need surviving the first few months too! Don’t be afraid to add items for yourself to your registry:

  • A massive insulated water bottle for long nursing sessions
  • A subscription to a meal delivery service or food delivery gift cards
  • Comfortable loungewear or a premium bathrobe

4. Use a Universal App

Don't limit yourself to one baby store. By using a universal wishlist tool, you can register for the crib from a specialty furniture store, the stroller from Amazon, and the custom nursery art from Etsy—all on one page.
