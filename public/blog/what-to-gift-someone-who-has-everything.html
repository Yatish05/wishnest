What to Gift Someone Who Has Everything (10 Real Ideas)

By WishNest • 6 min read

We all have that one friend or parent. If they want something, they just buy it immediately on Amazon. Shopping for them feels impossible. You can't buy them gadgets because they already have the newest version, and you can't buy them clothes because their taste is too specific.

So, what to gift someone who has everything? The trick is to pivot away from standard retail items and focus on upgrades, experiences, and perishables. Here are 10 foolproof ideas.

1. The "Upgrade" Everyday Items

Look at what they use every day, and buy the absurdly premium version of it. They might buy their own olive oil, but do they buy $40 finishing olive oil imported from Italy? They might have socks, but do they have merino wool running socks?

  • Truffle hot sauce or premium finishing salt
  • A high-end, heavy-duty phone charging cable (like Anker or Nomad)
  • A luxury hand soap set for their guest bathroom

2. Digital Subscriptions & Experiences

Physical objects take up space. Digital subscriptions do not. Pay for a year of a service they might not splurge on themselves:

  • A MasterClass annual subscription
  • Spotify Premium or YouTube Premium (offer to pay for their year)
  • Tickets to a local comedy show or wine tasting

3. High-End Consumables

If you give them something they can eat or drink, it won't sit in a closet forever. Instead of a generic fruit basket, get specific:

  • A bag of beans from a famous international coffee roaster
  • A box of artisanal, hand-painted chocolates
  • A premium bottle of a spirit they enjoy (like a rare Japanese whisky)

4. A Personalized Digital Wishlist

Sometimes the best approach is just to ask, but make it fun. Tell them you're using WishNest to keep track of birthdays this year, and send them a link to create their profile. Even people who "have everything" usually have a secret list of books or niche hobbies they want to explore!
